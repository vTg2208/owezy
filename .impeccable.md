## Design Context

### Users

Owezy serves India-first groups of friends and families coordinating shared trip expenses, usually on phones while travelling. Their primary jobs are adding an expense quickly, understanding who owes whom, proving a receipt when needed, and closing the trip without awkward arithmetic.

### Brand Personality

Calm, companionable, and quietly playful. Owezy should feel like a well-kept travel notebook rather than a finance dashboard or a generic startup template.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, paper-and-ink travel-led interface with warm mineral neutrals, deep forest text, and a restrained marigold accent. Typography should be characterful but highly legible. Avoid neon gradients, glass panels, repetitive floating cards, financial-trading aesthetics, and decorative motion.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next financial action obvious and reversible.
2. Keep money, people, and status readable at a glance on a phone.
3. Use color as a scarce semantic signal, never as decoration alone.
4. Prefer flat, well-spaced sections over nested cards and modals.
5. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard use, reduced motion, and 44px touch targets.
